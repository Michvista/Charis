# Accessories

Accessories complete an outfit and should be minimal, purposeful, and coordinated with the garment's metal and leather tones.

## Belts

- Match the belt leather to the shoes (brown with brown, black with black).
- The belt buckle metal should match the watch or other metal accents.
- Slim belts read dressier; wide belts read casual.

## Watches

- A metal or leather-strap watch is safe for almost any occasion.
- Match watch metal to other visible metal (belt buckle, rings, cufflinks).
- Avoid oversized sports watches with formal tailoring.

## Bags

- A structured bag or briefcase for business; a relaxed tote or crossbody for casual.
- Leather should match or complement the shoe color.
- Keep the bag proportional to the outfit — oversized bags swamp a tailored look.

## Scarves and Neckwear

- Silk scarves and ties dress an outfit up; knit and cotton scarves dress it down.
- Tie the scarf color to one existing tone in the outfit.
- Ties: wool or grenadine for texture, silk for sheen; width should match the lapel.

## Jewelry

- Formal: one or two refined pieces — cufflinks, a subtle ring, or a classic chain.
- Casual: minimal and personal; avoid mixing loud metals.
- When wearing multiple pieces, keep them in the same metal family.

## The Rule of Restraint

- One statement accessory per outfit is plenty.
- If every piece is loud, the outfit is noisy. Choose one focal accessory and keep the rest quiet.
