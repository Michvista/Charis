# Dress Codes

Understand the occasion's dress code before choosing any garment. The dress code sets the formality floor, the fabric expectations, and the footwear rules for the whole outfit.

## Formal (Black Tie / White Tie)

- Black tie requires a tuxedo or equivalent formal tailoring: a dark suit or tuxedo jacket, white dress shirt, polished black shoes.
- White tie is the most formal level: tailcoat, white bow tie, patent leather shoes.
- Jewelry should be minimal and refined — a classic watch or cufflinks, never casual sport pieces.
- Fabrics: wool, satin lapels, silk. Avoid denim, jersey, and athletic materials entirely.
- If the event is a formal gala or evening dinner, err on the side of a darker, more tailored silhouette.

## Semi-Formal (Cocktail / Business Formal)

- Cocktail attire sits between business and black tie. Men: dark suit, dress shirt, optional tie. Women: cocktail dress or elegant separates.
- Business formal means a full suit in a conservative color — navy, charcoal, or black.
- Shoes should be closed-toe and polished; loafers and oxfords are safe choices.
- Fabric should look structured: wool, crepe, silk blends. Avoid overly casual cotton or linen for evening semi-formal.

## Smart Casual

- Smart casual allows relaxed tailoring: chinos or tailored trousers with a blazer, or a crisp shirt with dark jeans.
- The rule is "polished but not stiff." One unstructured element is acceptable (blazer over a tee, or loafers without socks).
- Footwear can be loafers, clean sneakers, or leather derbies — but never athletic trainers unless the event explicitly says so.

## Casual

- Casual allows denim, tees, knits, and sneakers. Still avoid torn or athletic-only pieces unless the occasion is explicitly sporty.
- Fit matters more than formality at the casual level: a well-fitting tee and clean sneakers read better than a baggy button-down.
- Shoes can be sneakers, espadrilles, or simple sandals depending on the setting.

## Weddings

- **Black-tie wedding**: full formal — tuxedo or dark suit, formal shoes, muted accessories.
- **Beach wedding**: light linen or cotton suits, no heavy wool; breathable fabrics; footwear optional in sand — loafers or espadrilles that can be removed. Keep colors light and airy.
- **Garden / daytime wedding**: light suits, pastels, breathable fabrics; flat or low heeled shoes that suit grass.
- **Evening / city wedding**: darker tailoring, cocktail dress or dark suit.
- Never wear white as a guest to a Western wedding (reserved for the couple).

## Business / Office

- Business formal: tailored suit and tie or equivalent.
- Business casual: chinos or dark denim, collared shirts, knitwear; no athletic wear.
- Keep colors professional — navy, grey, charcoal, earth tones — with one accent piece allowed.
- Footwear should be closed and polished; avoid open-toed or athletic shoes in a corporate setting.
