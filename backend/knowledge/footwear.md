# Footwear

Footwear anchors an outfit and must match the occasion's formality. The wrong shoe can lower or raise the entire look regardless of the garments.

## Formal Footwear

- Oxfords, derbies, and polished loafers for business and formal events.
- Black or dark brown leather for evening; patent leather for black tie.
- Keep them polished and closed-toe for formal occasions.

## Smart Casual Footwear

- Loafers, penny loafers, suede derbies, and minimal white sneakers.
- Works with chinos, tailored trousers, and dark denim.
- Sneakers must be clean and minimal — no athletic trainers.

## Casual Footwear

- Sneakers, canvas shoes, espadrilles, and simple sandals.
- Pair with denim, shorts, and relaxed knits.
- Match the shoe color to the outfit's neutral base.

## Seasonal Footwear

- Warm weather: loafers, espadrilles, sandals, white sneakers — breathable.
- Cold weather: boots, chukkas, derbies — waterproof and insulated when needed.

## Footwear and Color Coordination

- Match shoes to the darkest neutral in the outfit for cohesion (black shoes with black or charcoal, brown shoes with tan or earth tones).
- Suede reads slightly more casual than polished leather at the same formality.
- Belt and shoes should share a leather tone — brown with brown, black with black.

## Fit and Comfort

- Shoes should fit correctly — this is the single most important factor for long events.
- Break in new shoes before a formal occasion or long wedding.
- When attending events with walking or standing, choose comfort-tuned soles over fashion-first options.
