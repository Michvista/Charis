# Seasonal Dressing

Dressing for the season is about temperature, light, and context. Choose fabrics and layers that match the climate while keeping the occasion's formality intact.

## Spring

- Transitional weather: light layers — trench coats, denim jackets, cardigans.
- Fabrics: cotton, chino, light knits, and mid-weight linen.
- Colors: pastels, sage, camel, and warm neutrals.

## Summer

- Prioritize breathability: linen, cotton, seersucker, and light blends.
- Loose, structured tailoring keeps you cool without looking sloppy.
- Footwear: loafers, espadrilles, clean white sneakers, or sandals where appropriate.
- Colors: lighter tones — white, sand, sky blue, sage; dark colors absorb heat.

## Autumn / Fall

- Layering begins: knits, flannel, lightweight wool, jackets.
- Fabrics: corduroy, tweed, wool, and heavier cotton.
- Colors: earth tones — olive, rust, camel, burgundy, mustard.
- Footwear: boots, derbies, and suede shoes.

## Winter

- Insulation is the priority: wool, cashmere, flannel, and down or padded outerwear.
- Layer smartly — base, mid, and outer — rather than relying on one heavy piece.
- Accessories: scarves, gloves, and hats in coordinated neutrals.
- Colors: deep navy, charcoal, black, and warm browns; keep accents muted.

## Season + Occasion

- A formal event in summer calls for lightweight formal fabrics (tropical wool, linen suiting) rather than heavy wool.
- A casual event in winter calls for warm knits and boots while still avoiding athletic-only gear.
- Match the season first, then the formality; the two together determine the correct capsule.
